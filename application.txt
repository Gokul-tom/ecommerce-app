E-commerce Application

Product Catalog
-  Product listing is available
-  Product name and price are displayed
-  Product category is displayed
-  Product availability status is displayed

Product search:
-  Product can be displayed
-  Search results are displayed

Pricing 
-  Product prices can be updated
-  Updated prices are reflected in the product catalog

Wishlist
-  Product can be added
-  Wishlist items can be viewed separetely


Customer support
-   Customers can view support information
-   Support requests can be submitted

Customer notification:
-  Customer can receive order status notifications.
-  Notification preferences can be managed by the customers.


Order tracking
-  Customer can view order status
-  Order status is updated as the order progresses
